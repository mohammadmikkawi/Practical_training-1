Our Story

Founded in 1999 in the heart of Nablus, Bin Makawi began as a small family roastery dedicated to preserving traditional Palestinian coffee culture. What started with a single hand‑crank roaster has grown into a modern craft facility, yet our commitment to freshness and flavor remains unchanged.

Every batch is roasted on‑site, ground to order, and shipped the same day. We source ethically, support local farmers, and celebrate the rich heritage of Levantine spices and teas.
